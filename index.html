Trends Shaping LKN — June 2026
📉
NC Job Growth Is Slowing — And That Matters Here
North Carolina's year-over-year job growth cooled to just 0.8% in early 2026. Slower growth means the competition for skilled talent intensifies locally. Workforce readiness and soft skills training are no longer nice-to-haves — they are the competitive edge.
WORKFORCE
💸
Wages Losing the Race to Cost of Living
NC wages grew 3.4% mid-2024 to mid-2025 — but housing costs grew 3.8% and energy costs surged 6%. This affordability gap directly impacts workforce availability for LKN employers and who can afford to live here.
ECONOMICS
🏥
Duke Health's $284M Acquisition Anchors LKN Identity
Duke Health's 2025 acquisition of Lake Norman Regional Medical Center signals LKN's growing status as a regional healthcare hub — meaning more jobs, more credential demand, and more opportunity for community health partnerships.
HEALTHCARE · ECONOMY
🤝
Nonprofit Collaboration Is the 2026 Survival Strategy
Research from the Sustained Collaboration Network finds 73% of nonprofits that collaborate — through shared programs, joint initiatives, or strategic alliances — achieve expanded services, increased funding, and better outcomes. Going it alone is a risk most cannot afford.
NONPROFITS
🤖
AI Governance Is the #1 Compliance Issue for Every Sector
From HR hiring tools to educational platforms to legal documentation, AI is embedded everywhere. June's Visionary Circle speaker Nils Tracy addresses why compliance, security, and governance frameworks must be built NOW — before exposure, not after.
AI · COMPLIANCE
